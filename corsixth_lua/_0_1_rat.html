function Rat:_walkToNextTile()

Walk the rat one tile further along its path, mirroring how humanoids move (see `action_walk_raw`). The rat animates across a single tile over `walk_quantity` ticks, then this runs again for the following tile until the destination hole is reached. Keeping the tile position authoritative means the rat stays clickable and detectable on the entity map.
